# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place to learn and teach the theory of computation, for people of every background and level of experience. Beginners’ questions are always on-topic.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and explanations, not people.
- Explain *why* when you correct something — this is a learning project.
- Credit others’ work and sources.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others’ private information without permission.
- Submitting content you do not have the right to share (e.g. copied textbook passages).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently block contributors who repeatedly do so.

Report problems by opening a confidential [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/imhasnainizhar/automata-theory/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
